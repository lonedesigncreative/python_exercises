> 1. `number1 = 4.5`

>
> 3. `sum_result = number1 + number2` 
> - Calculate the sum of number1 and number2 and store the result in the variable sum_result. This will perform the addition operation and give us the total of the two numbers.
> 
> 4. `print(f"The sum is: {sum_result}")` 
> - Print the result of the sum operation to the console. The output will display the sum of number1 and number2 in a formatted string. Alternatively, we can directly print the sum without storing it in a variable, as shown below.
> 
> 5. `print(f"Sum: {number1 + number2}")`
> - Calculate and print the sum of number1 and number2 in a single line. This will perform the addition operation and display the result directly in the output without needing an intermediate variable to store the sum.
> 
> 6. `subtraction = number1 - number2`
> - Calculate the difference between number1 and number2 and store the result in the variable subtraction. This will perform the subtraction operation and give us the result of number1 minus number2

> - Adding two numbers
> 
> 8. `print(f"Subtraction: {subtraction}")`
> -Subtracting number2 from number1

> -  Calculate the product of number1 and number2 and store the result in the variable multiplication. This will perform the multiplication operation and give us the result of number1 multiplied by number2.
> 
> 10. `print(f"Multiplication: {multiplication}")`
> - Multiplying two numbers
> 
> 11. `division = number1 / number2`
> - Calculate the quotient of number1 divided by number2 and store the result in the variable division. This will perform the division operation and give us the result of number1 divided by number2.
> 
> 12. `print(f"Division: {division}")`
> - Division by zero will raise an error
> 
> 13. `remainder = number1 % number2`
> - Calculate the remainder of number1 divided by number2 using the modulus operator (%) and store the result in the variable remainder. This will give us the value that is left over after dividing number1 by number2.
> 
> 14. `print(f"Remainder: {remainder}")`
> -Modulo operator gives the remainder of the division of number1 by number2
